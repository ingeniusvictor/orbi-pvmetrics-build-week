# Climate Recovery Claims Register

Status: CR-01 domain foundation

Scope: AI for Climate Innovation Factory 2026 Competition Edition

This register controls statements about the current repository state. It does
not replace evidence or authorize operational, commercial, or climate claims.

## Permitted claims

- Climate Recovery Domain Foundation has been implemented.
- The system can represent recoverable loss hypotheses.
- The system distinguishes measured, derived, estimated, simulated, and
  projected data.
- Synthetic demonstration cases are available.
- Human review and uncertainty are represented in the domain model.
- Double-counting risks can be flagged for review.
- Configurable emission-factor contracts and fail-closed validation are
  represented.
- Synthetic recovery and climate-impact estimates cannot be labeled verified.

## Prohibited claims at this stage

- Climate Recovery is operational in production.
- ORBI has recovered real MWh.
- ORBI has avoided verified emissions.
- The platform provides definitive diagnoses.
- The platform is connected to live SCADA.
- The platform dispatches maintenance crews.
- The platform controls BESS or grid assets.
- The climate estimates are externally validated.
- The system has paying customers or pilots, unless separately proven.
- The AI model is statistically calibrated, unless separately proven.
- The fictional emission factor represents a real grid, region, or universal
  value.
- A simulated recovery scenario is a guaranteed prediction.

## Usage rule

Every future claim must retain the distinction among source data, inference,
simulation, projection, human review, and verification. Synthetic fixtures may
demonstrate contract behavior only; they are not evidence of operational or
climate outcomes.
